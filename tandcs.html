RJC Audio provides professional online audio mixing and mastering services. The scope of work will be defined and agreed upon before any work begins, typically via written communication (e.g. email or invoice).

2. Delivery and Turnaround

Turnaround time is typically 5 business days from the date all project materials (audio stems, reference tracks) and payment are received, unless a rush delivery or alternative timeline has been mutually agreed in writing.

3. Revisions

  • First Mix Free: Includes up to 3 rounds of revisions on the demo snippet.
  • Pro Mix: Includes up to 3 rounds of mix revisions via the Samply platform.
  • Elite Mix: Includes unlimited mix revisions until client satisfaction.

Revisions apply to level adjustments, EQ/compression modifications, effect tweaks, and minor timing changes. Re-recording replacement tracks or radical arrangement overhauls after mixing has commenced may incur additional stem-handling fees.
